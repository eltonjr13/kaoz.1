**Raccoon Radar** — a porch camera that identifies nighttime visitors and publishes a nightly leaderboard of the neighborhood's most prolific raccoons.

- Trigger clip capture on motion after dark, filtering out cats, possums, and wind.
- Re-identify individual raccoons by mask markings so each gets a persistent name.
- Score every visit for mischief: tipped bins, stolen suet, dramatic tree escapes.
- Serve a tiny web page with tonight's standings and all-time records.
- Text a morning digest: who came, what they did, who's currently winning.
